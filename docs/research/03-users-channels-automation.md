# Users, channels and safe automation (research, Oct 30, 2026)

Items marked **[unconfirmed]** came from secondary or single sources, or could not be fetched (reddit.com and grantprofessionals.org blocked direct fetches).

### C1. The consultant / freelance grant writer

**How many.** GPA reports 4,363 members as of Dec 31, 2025, across 54 chapters ([GPA FAQ](https://grantprofessionals.org/page/FAQ)). GPA's 2023 compensation survey: 13% full-time consultants, 12% part-time with another job, 6% part-time with no other job, roughly 31% consult ([AFP summary](https://careers.afpglobal.org/career-resources/on-the-job-7/grant-writing-a-practical-guide-for-fundraising-113)). BLS does not track grant writers; the closest code is Fundraisers, 125,900 employed in 2023 ([BLS OOH](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/business-and-financial/fundraisers.htm)). Upwork shows 2,201 grant-writing projects; Fiverr 3,100+ to 4,193 service listings.

**Client load.** No survey figure. Instrumentl shows a testimonial "Manages 10+ clients"; a Capterra reviewer manages 15–20 nonprofits at once. A new writer takes 15–25 hours per full proposal, an experienced one 5–10 ([Funding for Good](https://fundingforgood.org/are-you-charging-enough-for-grant-writing-services/)); about 20 billable hours in a 40-hour week ([Grant Writing Made Easy](https://www.grantwritingmadeeasy.com/behind-the-scenes-with-a-grant-writing-consultant/)). That implies roughly 3–8 active clients for a solo full-timer.

**How they charge.** GPA 2023: median consultant billing rate $80/hr (mean $91.29); full-time consultants median $100/hr. 2026 guides: $50–$150/hr, senior federal specialists $150–$250+ ([freelancerates.net](https://www.freelancerates.net/grant-writer)); LOIs $400–$1,200; foundation proposals $1,500–$5,000; federal $5,000–$15,000+ ([winagrant](https://winagrant.com/blog/how-much-does-a-grant-writer-cost)); retainers $2,000–$6,000/month ([biztoolkit](https://www.biztoolkit.co/post/freelance-grant-writer-rates-in-2026-per-project-fees)). Percentage-of-award pay is an ethics violation under the GPA Code; commissions and finder's fees are barred ([GPA ethics](https://grantprofessionals.org/page/ethics); [AFP Standard 21](https://afpglobal.org/ethical-standard-deep-dive-standard-21)).

**Tools today.** Instrumentl: separate profile per client, a "Viewing:" dropdown, task assignment, status column, exports, emailing clients ([Instrumentl](https://www.instrumentl.com/blog/grant-consulting-client-management)). GrantHub and GrantHub Pro sunset Jan 31, 2026; Instrumentl's migration page: "103 GrantHub users have already moved". Low-cost alternatives: GEMS (free tier; Pro $69), Grant Holster ($10/mo), Grant Frog ($99/mo); GEMS advertises "Multi-Client Portfolio Management… giving each client access only to their specific data" ([Grant Frog roundup](https://grantfrog.com/5-best-grants-management-software/)). Spreadsheets remain the default (Smartsheet, GrantLink and others publish free trackers). Notion client-portal templates are sold on Gumroad. Invoicing: Wave (free), Harvest, Toggl.

**Pain points.**
1. Getting information from clients on time is "the hardest aspect of this work" ([Fundraising HayDay](https://fundraisinghayday.podbean.com/e/getting-the-information-you-need-part-2-tools-to-get-the-job-done)); "clients not getting information to me on time, resulting in some last-minute panic" ([DH Leonard](https://www.dhleonardconsulting.com/i-still-have-challenges-with-clients-not-getting-information-to-me-on-time-resulting-in-some-last-minute-panic/)).
2. Competing deadlines across clients; the fix is "one unified deadline view" ([Grantboost](https://grantboost.io/blog/freelance-grant-writer-toolkit/)); "impossible cycle of multitasking," login management for multiple accounts ([Instrumentl](https://www.instrumentl.com/blog/grant-consulting-client-management)).
3. Scope creep and "access creep" ([Grant Writing Made Easy](https://www.grantwritingmadeeasy.com/grant-contracting-scope-creep-5-clauses-that-protect-your-time-and-your-profit/)).
4. Handoff and ownership: the nonprofit should own narrative, boilerplate, budgets and research "in editable formats, delivered at engagement end"; "nobody owns the reporting calendar" ([pricinglink](https://pricinglink.com/knowledge-base/grant-writing-services-for-nonprofits/grant-writing-service-contract-essentials/)); GPA says agreements should spell out ownership ([GPA](https://grantprofessionals.org/news/593249/Put-It-in-Writing.htm)).
5. Underbilling from reconstructing hours after the fact; late payments.
6. Clients keep parallel trackers, so consultants get added as a seat on the client's account.

### C2. The single-org grants manager
Titles: Grants Manager, Grants Coordinator, Development Director doing grants. Salary: PayScale $66,103; Salary.com $87,237; Zippia $60,700; GPA staff median $73,000. A ~$1M-budget org with a $300–500K grants portfolio hires a coordinator; 25–30 active grants becomes "too much" for one person ([Bridgespan](https://www.bridgespan.org/insights/should-create-director-development-position)). Grants tracked: ED part-time 3–5; writer-plus-manager 8–12; full-time post-award manager 8–15 per $1–2M ([Grantsights](https://grantsights.com/blog/how-to-build-a-grant-funding-portfolio)). Spreadsheets break at ~5–10 active grants. Complaints: things "fall through the cracks"; wants "a tool that reminds her"; yearly spreadsheet rebuilds; Instrumentl pricing "a stretch for very small nonprofits".

**Communities.** r/nonprofit does not allow self-promotion ([Coyote Communications](https://www.coyotecommunications.com/outreach/reddit.shtml)); r/grantwriting rules not retrievable; Nonprofit Happy Hour (Facebook) ~30K–40K members, rules not found; GPA GrantZone members-only; AFP Connect prohibits self-promotion.

**Launch venues.** Show HN: must be tryable; no landing pages or sign-up pages; "Please don't ask friends to upvote"; voting-ring detector ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html)). Product Hunt: makers hunt their own product; company accounts prohibited; no upvote asks; relaunch only on major iterations ([PH FAQ](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/faqs)); Plausible's PH day: 1,000 visitors, 15 trials. Indie Hackers: liberal about self-promotion. OpenAlternative: free, dofollow (unconfirmed). awesome-selfhosted: FOSS licence; "first released more than 4 months ago"; tagged releases required; "Machine/LLM-generated contributions are not allowed and will result in a ban" ([CONTRIBUTING](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/awesome-selfhosted/awesome-selfhosted-data/master/CONTRIBUTING.md)). GitHub topics: up to 20 per repo.

### C4. Open-source tools that got adoption
CiviCRM (2005): downloads on SourceForge, built by watching what nonprofits did, sustained by consultancies selling services; now 10,000+ orgs. Ushahidi (2008): a blog post asked for help; reach came from the founders' existing audiences. Open Collective (2015): created its own fiscal-host nonprofits to showcase the platform. Grantmakers.io: one person, GitHub Pages, "no limits on views, no limited free trials, no frustrating pop-ups". ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer (2013): became one of ProPublica's most popular databases, added alerts and an API. Open-source SaaS: Plausible's biggest driver was HN blog posts (25,000 visitors in a day), "open source alternative to Google Analytics" positioning, "never paid to advertise", $1M ARR by June 2022 ([Plausible](https://plausible.io/blog/open-source-saas)). Cal.com: PH launch #1 of the month, 0 to ~5,000 stars in weeks. Twenty: Launch HN with two months of features. Formbricks: stars "mainly from Product Hunt + Hackernews launch". Documenso: PH. Dub: "open-source alternative to Bitly". Common playbook: name the incumbent, make the free thing fully usable with no gate, write data-driven posts for HN, treat PH as one spike, get into alternative directories.

### C5. Programmatic SEO in this niche
Instrumentl browse pages: 37,000+ grants by focus area, all 50 states, cities; ungated with a trial CTA; 450K–504K funder profiles. GrantWatch: state subdomains (texas.grantwatch.com, 1,988 grants) and category pages, detail paywalled. OpenGrants: free federal listings plus a listicle cluster. Grants.gov ranks #1 for "grants" (~17K visits/mo from that term). Deadline calendars already exist (GrantedAI's free filterable federal calendar with reminders; GrantsDue's daily refresh and embeddable widget). Lead magnets: Instrumentl gates a calendar template behind email but leaves its tracker ungated. No published conversion data. Verdict: templates and calendars are table stakes; the differentiator is "import this template into a working tracker in one click" and per-state, per-cause "deadlines this month" pages from Grants.gov data.

### C6. Pricing precedents
Stripe 2.9% + 30¢ on US cards, so $1 nets ~$0.67; Stripe Billing adds ~0.5–0.7%; minimum charge $0.50; annual billing ($12 once) cuts the fixed fee to 2.5%. The "penny gap": the largest gap is between free and one cent ([Kopelman](https://redeye.firstround.com/2007/03/the_first_penny.html)). Tiny subscriptions can work at volume (a solo habit app with ~25,100 subscribers at $1–2/mo). Tip jars: Buy Me a Coffee 5%; Ko-fi 0% on donations. Webinar-as-benefit: GPA's $235 membership includes monthly webinars; GPA Next Level $399/$449 for 12 classes; Candid trainings free; Bloomerang webinars free; NonprofitReady free; paid grant-writer communities $10–$500/mo. A $1/month webinar undercuts everything paid and competes with free Candid; the pitch must be specificity.

| GitHub releases + topics | Yes | Up to 20 topics; tagged releases satisfy awesome-selfhosted |
| LinkedIn Page posts | Yes, after approval | Community Management API app review; verified website and matching Page |
| X | Yes, metered | Pay-per-use since Feb 6, 2026; ~$0.01–0.015 per post, URL posts reportedly $0.20 |
| Bluesky | Yes, free | App password; 5,000 points/hr, 35,000/day |
| Mastodon | Yes, free | 300 requests / 5 min; mark the account as a bot |
| Reddit | No | Self-service API closed Nov 11, 2025; r/nonprofit bans self-promotion |
| Product Hunt | Manual | Personal account, no upvote asks |
| Show HN | Manual | Tryable product, no vote asks |
| awesome lists | Manual | LLM-written PRs banned |
| Capterra/G2/TechSoup/blog lists | Manual forms, once | See C3 |

**Proposed weekly zero-touch cadence.** Nightly: pull Grants.gov, regenerate state × cause deadline pages and sitemap. Monday: auto-publish "Grant deadlines this month" (first Monday) or "Closing this week"; cross-post via Bluesky, Mastodon, LinkedIn Page (once approved). Wednesday: RSS → email digest. On tag: GitHub release notes → blog → social. Monthly, human, ~1 hour: live webinar, one Indie Hackers update, one reply to a relevant r/nonprofit thread with no link. Once, human: Capterra/G2/TechSoup/FreeForNonprofits submissions; awesome-list PR by hand after 4 months; Show HN when a sign-up-free demo exists; Product Hunt after that.
