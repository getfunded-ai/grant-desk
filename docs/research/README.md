# Research

Done on 2026-10-30 by three research agents and one repo survey, before the plan was written. Every
figure carries its source. Items marked unconfirmed came from a single or secondary source.

- [01-grant-management-platforms.md](01-grant-management-platforms.md): what a grant management platform
  is, the seeker's lifecycle, 2 CFR 200 rules, every seeker-side product with prices, the feature matrix,
  the ten most repeated complaints, what "meaningfully better" means, market size.
- [02-government-grant-data.md](02-government-grant-data.md): Grants.gov search2, fetchOpportunity and the
  XML extract; Simpler.Grants.gov and CommonGrants; System-to-System; SAM.gov; USAspending; SBIR.gov and
  the DOE process; state and local portals; foundation data sources; email and calendar.
- [03-users-channels-automation.md](03-users-channels-automation.md): consultant grant writers, single-org
  grant managers, search terms, directories, communities, launch venues, open-source growth playbooks,
  programmatic SEO, pricing precedents, safe automation by channel.
