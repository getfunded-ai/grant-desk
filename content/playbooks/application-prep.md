# Preparing an application

## What to read

The `grants` list shows what the organization is applying for and the next deadline. The
`deadlines` list shows everything else due in the same weeks. Read the opportunity's published
guidelines (the person names the source, or the grant links to a catalog opportunity).

## What to produce

- `knowledge_fact` items about the opportunity: required attachments, page limits, budget rules,
  match requirements, review criteria, submission method, the exact deadline and time zone. Each
  cites the guideline page or document.
- `task` items that break the application into steps, each with a date that leaves room before the
  deadline, in the order they must happen.
- `stage_move` items only when the record shows the work has reached the next stage, with the
  evidence. A person accepts a stage move on the grant record page.
- `lesson` items about how this organization prepares applications.

## What counts as a lesson

What took longer than planned, which attachment needed another person's sign-off, which requirement
was missed last time, with evidence from the packet or the guidelines.

## Rules that always apply

Never write that the organization is eligible. Record what the guidelines say and let a person
decide. Unknown dates and amounts stay "Not available".
