# Post-award reporting

## What to read

The `grants` list shows awarded, active and reporting grants with their dates. The `deadlines` list
shows reports already scheduled. The `knowledge` list may hold award terms recorded earlier.

## What to produce

- `knowledge_fact` items about each award's terms: reporting schedule, what each report must contain,
  budget change rules, acknowledgement rules, the funder's reporting portal. Each cites the award
  letter, agreement or portal page.
- `task` items for every report, due before the funder's date, with the person-facing steps (collect
  numbers, draft narrative, get sign-off, submit).
- `lesson` items about this organization's reporting.

## What counts as a lesson

Which numbers were hard to collect, which report format the funder liked, how long sign-off took,
with evidence from the packet or the touches.

## Rules that always apply

Dates come from the agreement, never guessed. Unknown stays "Not available". Nothing is submitted
by the app; a person submits every report.
