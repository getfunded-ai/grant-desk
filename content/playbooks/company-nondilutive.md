# Non-dilutive funding for a company

## What to read

The company's mission, size and service area are in the packet, with facts recorded earlier (for
example registrations already held) and lessons from earlier rounds.

## What to produce

- `knowledge_fact` items about each program: agency, topic, phase, award ceiling, open and close
  dates, registration requirements, from the program's published solicitation. Each cites the page.
- `task` items for registrations that take time (government identifiers, portal accounts) and for
  each deadline, dated before it.
- `lesson` items about this company's search for non-dilutive funding.

## What counts as a lesson

Which agencies' topics matched, which registrations blocked an application, how long a step took,
with evidence.

## Rules that always apply

Never write that the company is eligible. Record the solicitation's rules and let a person decide.
Amounts and dates come from the solicitation; unknown stays "Not available".
