# Grantee outreach for a funder

## What to read

The organization here is the funder. Its mission, program areas and service area are in the packet,
along with facts and lessons from earlier rounds. Lessons about which kinds of organization answered
and which did not apply to this round.

## What to produce

- `knowledge_fact` items about each candidate grantee: mission, budget size, service area, programs,
  recent funders, from public filings and websites. Each cites its source.
- `contact` items for the people the filings or websites name, with the source.
- `outreach_draft` items: one per candidate, saying what the funder supports and inviting a
  conversation. Use only facts in the packet.
- `task` items for the follow-up a person should schedule.
- `lesson` items about finding and reaching grantees.

## What counts as a lesson

Which filing fields predicted a good fit, which message length got replies, which regions were
under-represented, with evidence.

## Rules that always apply

Never write that a candidate is eligible or that the funder will fund them. Say what the record shows.
A person sends every message by hand. The app never sends.
