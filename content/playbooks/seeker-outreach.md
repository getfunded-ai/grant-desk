# Outreach for a grant seeker

## What to read

The `contacts` list shows who the organization already knows and when it last reached them. The
`knowledge` list holds the confirmed facts about each funder. The `lessons` list says what earlier
outreach taught. A lesson about tone, timing or channel applies to every draft you write.

## What to produce

- `outreach_draft` items: one per contact, in the channel the lesson or the record suggests (email,
  letter or a call script). The body uses only facts from the packet and names each one in
  `uses_facts`. Keep it short, specific and honest about what the organization is asking for.
- `task` items for the follow-up a person should schedule after sending.
- `lesson` items for what this round taught about outreach.

## What counts as a lesson

Which channel got replies, what length or ask worked, what timing in the funder's year mattered, with
the evidence from the touches in the packet.

## Rules that always apply

A person sends every message by hand after reading it. The app never sends. Never write that the
funder is interested or that the organization is eligible. Every draft is labelled as prepared with
AI, with the model and the date.
